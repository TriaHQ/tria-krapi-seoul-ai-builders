# Tria Affiliate Program Guide (ideathon edition)

*A simplified, fictional version of the program, made for this event. Rates and prices are not Tria's real terms.*

## How it works

As a Tria affiliate, you share your referral link. People who sign up through it become **your referrals**. When a referral buys a Tria product, you earn a commission.

You can also recruit other people to become affiliates. They are **your recruits**. When *their* referrals buy something, you earn a smaller commission too.

## Products

| Product | Price |
|---|---|
| Premium Card (`premium_card`) | $99 |
| Signature Card (`signature_card`) | $249 |
| Membership (`membership`) | $49 |

## Commission levels

| Level | When | Rate |
|---|---|---|
| **Level 1** | Your own referral buys something | Depends on your tier (below) |
| **Level 2** | A referral of one of your recruits buys something | Flat **5%**, whatever your tier |

Level 2 goes up one level only: you earn from your recruits' referrals, not from your recruits' recruits.

### Example

Your referral buys a Signature Card while you are Silver. You earn Level 1: $249 × 15% = **$37.35**.

If instead the buyer was referred by one of your recruits, your recruit earns Level 1 at their own tier, and you earn Level 2: $249 × 5% = **$12.45**.

## Tiers

Your tier sets your Level 1 rate. It is decided at the start of each month from your **paid direct sales in the previous month**, meaning purchases by your own referrals that were not refunded.

| Tier | Paid direct sales last month | Level 1 rate |
|---|---|---|
| Bronze | 0–4 | 10% |
| Silver | 5–14 | 15% |
| Gold | 15 or more | 20% |

- New affiliates, and everyone in the first month of the program (April 2026), start at **Bronze**.
- Your tier stays the same for the whole month, even if you pass a threshold mid-month. Hitting the threshold this month raises your tier **next** month.
- Level 2 sales do not count towards your tier.

## Refunds

If a purchase is refunded, every commission from it (Level 1 and Level 2) is **reversed** and no longer counts as earnings. A refunded purchase also does not count towards your tier.

## FAQ

**When does my tier change?**
On the 1st of each month, based on the month before.

**I'm Silver with 12 sales this month. What do I need for Gold?**
3 more paid direct sales this month makes you Gold next month.

**Why is my commission on the same product different in different months?**
Your tier, and so your Level 1 rate, may have been different in those months.

**Do my recruits' sales help my tier?**
No. Only your own referrals' purchases count.
